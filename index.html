Political Biography

The Making of a Political Star

From a CA firm to India's youngest Rajya Sabha MP — Raghav Chadha's rise was meteoric, ambitious, and controversial.

1988 · New Delhi
Born into a Middle-Class Delhi Family
Born on November 11, 1988, Chadha attended Modern School and went on to earn a Bachelor's in Commerce from Delhi University (2009) and qualified as a Chartered Accountant from ICAI (2011). He briefly worked at Deloitte and Grant Thornton before politics pulled him in.
Early Life
2012
Joins AAP at Its Very Birth
One of the earliest members of Arvind Kejriwal's Aam Aadmi Party, joining when it was formed out of the India Against Corruption movement. His financial background made him a natural fit for treasury and fundraising operations.
AAP Founding Member
2015
Appointed National Treasurer at Age 26
After AAP's historic landslide in Delhi (67 of 70 seats), Chadha was named the party's National Treasurer — the youngest ever in a major Indian political party. He designed the crowdfunding model that made AAP financially independent.
National Treasurer
2019
Contested Lok Sabha from South Delhi — Lost
His first electoral contest from the South Delhi Lok Sabha constituency. He lost to BJP's Ramesh Bidhuri, but the campaign firmly put him on the national political map as a young, articulate face of AAP.
First Election
2020
Elected MLA from Rajinder Nagar, Delhi
Won the Rajinder Nagar Assembly seat in the 2020 Delhi elections, cementing his grassroots political credentials while remaining one of the most prominent AAP spokespersons on national television.
Delhi MLA
2022
Youngest Rajya Sabha MP in India's History
On March 21, 2022, AAP nominated Chadha to the Rajya Sabha from Punjab — making him the youngest sitting MP in India's Upper House at just 33. He became the face of AAP's national ambitions.
Historic Record
2023
Marries Parineeti Chopra — Celebrity Politician
In September 2023, Chadha married Bollywood actress Parineeti Chopra in a lavish ceremony in Udaipur. The wedding made him one of India's most-followed politicians on social media. He was also suspended from Rajya Sabha briefly during this period over a privileges committee controversy.
Personal Milestone
April 2, 2026
Removed as AAP's Deputy Leader in Rajya Sabha
AAP suddenly removed Chadha as its Deputy Leader in the Rajya Sabha, replacing him with Ashok Kumar Mittal. The official reason cited was his "prolonged absence" from party events — widely seen as a public humiliation.
Demotion
April 24, 2026
Led Mass Exodus — Joins BJP with 6 MPs
